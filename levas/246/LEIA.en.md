- `soltar_suspensoes_guardadas()` = 0x5567d0 (discipline.cpp, thiscall, ret 4): removes from the held list (+0x1c) the items of the person while the +0x1c counter of her record is positive;
- `tirar_ofertas_pendentes()` = 0x4ccbf0 (contract_manager.cpp, thiscall, ret 4): with pending offers (+8 of the 79-byte record), unmarks the agreed contract (0x8c5430) and removes the person offers (+0x34) until the byte is zero;
- `marcar_decisao_da_transferencia()` = 0x4cc930 (contract_manager.cpp, thiscall, ret 4): marks (+0x62) the first offer of the person and club still undecided and schedules the decision (0x4cd230); returns ability / 500 > club reputation;
- `noticia_para_todos_os_tecnicos()` = 0x786780 (news.cpp, thiscall, ret 4): all scope (+0xde = 4) and delivery (0x785d40) to every active human manager; 1, or 0 on the first refusal;
- `noticia_para_os_tecnicos_da_divisao()` = 0x7862d0 (news.cpp, thiscall, ret 8): division scope (+0xde = 1) and delivery to every active human manager with a club in the division (+0x57); 1, or 0 on the first refusal;
- `noticia_para_os_tecnicos_do_pais()` = 0x7863f0 (news.cpp, thiscall, ret 8): nation scope (+0xde = 2) and delivery to every active human manager whose club or national side belongs to the nation (+0x53); returns 0;
- `oferta_bate_o_recorde()` = 0x4486c0 (club_records.cpp, thiscall, ret 8): the offer fee passes 50 × reputation + 500,000 (and reputation² × 0x967880 when required) and the record (+0x313) of the buyer all-time records;
- `condicao_do_jogador()` = 0x62c590 (injury.cpp, thiscall, ret 8): fitness × condition / 10,000 (minimum 100, maximum 9000 while recovering), or as a percentage (maximum 100);
- `limite_de_escalacao()` = 0x8cf7e0 (transfer_manager.cpp, thiscall, ret 8): from the match restriction table (0x8cf6b0), the first one present: type at +0, count at +5 (and the age at +1 for type 3);
- `limpar_partidas_assistidas()` = 0x5fe8d0 (human_manager.cpp, thiscall): clears (79 bytes) the watched fixture of each active human manager who may no longer watch it (0x5fe700); returns 1;
- `alvos_dos_olheiros_do_clube()` = 0x7f9690 (scout_manager.cpp, thiscall, ret 8): empties the list and adds the person of each scout request of the club; returns whether there is any;
- `trocar_olheiro_da_busca()` = 0x7ae8a0 (player_search.cpp, thiscall, ret 4): new scout: stores the ids (0x7e4920) or −1, and the nation, region and competition assignments go back to −1; returns 1;
- `diretoria_depois_da_partida()` = 0x6951e0 (manager_manager.cpp, thiscall, ret 8): played match: the board confidence of each side with a manager changes (0x695240, home with 1);
- `disciplina_da_partida()` = 0x55ca00 (discipline.cpp, thiscall, ret 8): the competition ruling body judges the match (0x7e7ff0); without it, in friendlies, the league ruling bodies of both clubs;
- `entradas_do_clube_na_shortlist()` = 0x8420e0 (shortlist_manager.cpp, thiscall, ret 8): adds to the list every shortlist entry (8 bytes) of the club, found by binary search;
- `maior_oferta_pelo_jogador()` = 0x8cc930 (transfer_manager.cpp, thiscall, ret 8): of the offers for the player from the club (or flagged) in state 2, the highest (0x8e7a70); 0 when none;
