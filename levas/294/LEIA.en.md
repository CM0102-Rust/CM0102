- `write_player_media_story_news_text()` = 0x72d270 (media.cpp, thiscall, ret 0xc): writes the headline or the body of a media story about a player. The headline is one line by the story kind (slot 9), with the player's name (form 5) and the second person's (slots 11 to 14), and the links of the player, the second person, the club and its nation. The body reads the slots (0 the player, 1 to 3 the names, 4 the club, 5 the variant, 6 the outlet type, 9 the kind, 10 the reply, 16 to 18 the squad role, the age and the story bits, 23 to 26 the third person, the club manager or the human one, 28 with a match: the fixture record and the result; without one: the qualities, the position, the pundit and a fourth person) and writes: the outlet (the pundit's name when the media is local, that of the nation of the human manager on screen, and the story names one; otherwise the type, plain for the local media or with the adjective of the club's nation); the story by kind (left out, ability, complacency, contribution with the score and the other club, criticism by the bits of slot 18 when the variant is above 7 (without a bit, the variant becomes player % 8), commitment, indiscipline, purchase, the manager blamed, attitude, private life, transfer rumour, exclusive, too young, rumours, out of favour, past it, pressure, indiscipline that costs dear, poor run; the other kinds, the programmers' placeholder text); after a draw, how the club eventually won or lost (the result code); and the reply paragraph: the human manager's when he is the third person (support, criticise, ignore), otherwise the club manager's (support, criticise), by kind and, for support, by the squad role and the age. The final text is "%s\n\n%s" of the two, with the links of the persons whose names match, of the club's nation (name, short name and ten adjectives, to the national team), of the club, of the score (to the fixture, when Match_Stats finds it) and of the other club. Defects of the original kept: slot 14 is read twice and 15 never; the kind 0x10 foreign text passes the club's gender where its name goes; the private life above variant 6 writes nothing (the original prints its uninitialised buffer; the copy an empty text); the headline gives the names a size (0x190) larger than the buffers; the kind 0x11 headline divides by variant + 1;
- `write_player_praise_news_text()` = 0x744f80 (media.cpp, thiscall, ret 0xc): writes the headline or body of the story praising a player: reads the news slots (the player and his name ids, the club, the kind 0x29..0x34, a nation, the variant, the replying manager and his ids, the reply, the strengths, the position, a competition and, with the match record, its data; then a rival club, the great player with his ids, club and job, two nations, the outlet type and the pundit); the rival is dropped when of the human manager's nation (and player % 4 is not 0), and the press is "national" when the club is of that nation (otherwise "{nationality} ..."); the outlet by type (national or local newspaper, national or local radio station, magazine, sports paper, website) or the pundit's name; the great player by the ids or Bobby Charlton, Maradona, Pelé or Cruyff by (variant + player) % 4. Body: the first paragraph by kind and variant (promising player, future international, praised, call-up, on the rise, big move, attitude, world class, impresses at the club, world stage with two nations and the competition, and kind 0x34 with the match: score, stage, won or not, the rival who wants him, the signing date, the penalty decision), plus the manager's reply (in the second person for the human; by age against 24 and 34 or 36), joined with "%s\n\n%s". Headline: "Praise for {club} {position}" when variant % 8 = player % 7, otherwise by kind. Then the links: the match, the player, the manager and the great player when the ids match, the clubs, the club's nation and its national team under ten adjective forms, three nations and the competition. Without the match record in kind 0x34, the original's error box;
- `write_transfer_rumour_news_text()` = 0x755860 (media.cpp, thiscall, ret 0xc): the headline or body of the transfer rumour story: it reads the news slots (the player and his name, the bidding club, the kind of lead, the fee, the kind of agent line, the player's strengths and position, the agent and his name, a flag and the club list from slot 0x10 on, up to slot 0x32); the player's position and description; the story's nation by (clubs + agent + lead + club + player) % 6 with a bidding club (1-2 the club's, 3 the player's with reputation 6000 or more, 4-5 the first listed club's) and whether the human manager is of that nation. The headline: with lead 36, "unsettled" or "fuels transfer talk" by the player's id even or odd ("eyes ... move" without a bidder); otherwise by the player's id % 3, "transfer speculation", "rumour(s)", "lead chase for" or "linked with" the first club. The body: the lead by kind (0, 1, 36, the others), with or without a bidder and a fee (in kind 1 a foreign manager gets the nation or the adjective); the paragraph of the 2 to 5 clubs; the agent line (the human manager himself: three variants; the others quote the agent on the bidder); the links to the player, the agent, the bidder, the nation's national team (the name, the short name and the ten forms of the adjective) and the listed clubs;
- `post_team_media_story()` = 0x74b060 (media.cpp, thiscall, ret 0x18): posts the media story about a club or national team (news 0xbdf): nothing before the news start date, without a club, a division (unless a national team), a nation, a stadium and city (unless a national team), a manager or his sheet; a national team only for kinds 0x5a and 0x44; nothing if the story is already in the log or the person is a player below reputation 3500. The slots: the draw by kind (some kinds drop the person on a non-zero draw; the others become kind 0x63), the tone (a club up to 4500 + draw(1500): 2 or 4; otherwise by draw(100): 6, 3, 5, 0, 1 or 2), the nation, the club, the kind, the computer manager's answer (except for kinds 0x3e, 0x50, 0x51 and 0x5e), the manager's ambition and temperament, the competition (the national team's, only a finals one; the continental one; or the division), the manager and the person with their names, the game (its record), the variant, whether the club was the last one placed, the next hosts, the extra word, the person's job, whether he has caps, the shoot-out (won or lost) and a pundit. Delivery: a computer club on a non-zero draw(2), to its manager; otherwise by kind, to all, the continent, the division or the country (an English club from 6000 also to Scotland, a Scottish one from 6500 to England, the others also to the club's manager). Then the story log and, for the answered kinds at a computer club, the answer;
