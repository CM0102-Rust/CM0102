First generates the missing backroom staff (0x527240). For sizes other than 1 and 2, each nation keeps its best non-players (up to the nation reputation clamped to 5..15 × 10/15) and players (× 100/15). Moves the links of the other tables to the new row, compacts, creates the "virtual" staff (2% or 1% of those kept: born in 1982, nation of a drawn person, players without attributes), copies 10 drawn non-players into the last rows, recounts each nation's staff, moves the 16 human manager rows to the end and reallocates in place;

- `new_media()` = 0x72b1d0 (media.cpp, thiscall, ret 4): builds the media object: the transfer interest table and the story log empty (pointers, capacities and counts at 0) and, with a save, reads both from media.dat (load_media_dat; its answer is not used); returns the object;
