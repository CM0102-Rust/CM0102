  engine players/grid modelled; Portuguese strings almost gone; transmutes -14%; literal addresses -13%.
- Not improved: unsafe/SAFETY/static mut/tests identical; fin_prm.rs and league.rs headers exactly as reported; Portuguese locals

- Worse: the migration's cost is visible. `.addr()` rose 5,298 -> 7,721 and `.addr() + module::F` nearly doubled (769 -> 1,445);

  `read_T(x.addr() + rec::F)` with `x.f()` wherever records.rs has the accessor (most of the 1,445).
