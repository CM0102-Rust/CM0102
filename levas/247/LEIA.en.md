- `recordes_da_temporada()` = 0x4528d0 (club_records.cpp, thiscall, ret 8): the same for the season records (index +4, vector +0x10, capacity +0x14, count +0x18);

- `acrescentar_aposentadoria()` = 0x7a8510 (player_regen.cpp, thiscall, ret 4): appends the record fields to the 26-byte pending retirement table (+4), growing by 250;
- `guardar_vaga_para_regen()` = 0x7a87d0 (player_regen.cpp, thiscall, ret 4): the person enters the regen queue (+0x10) with the dates; the non-player data in the spare list (+0x1c, random nation if none) and the player data in the free pool (+0x3c);

- `esta_suspenso()` = 0x55d270 (discipline.cpp, thiscall, ret 0x10): suspended by the general body (0x7e85b0 with 1) or by the competition one (+0x50, with 0); in that case, in a human club, he leaves his slot among the 20 at +0x1e0;
- `escopo_das_regras_da_partida()` = 0x8d2fa0 (transfer_manager.cpp, thiscall, ret 0x10): reserves: the home nation and type 1; friendly: 0xff; otherwise the key nation of the competition (0x669470), the continent (+0x59) and type 4 (0, 1), 2 (3 to 7) or 1;
- `codigo_de_inelegibilidade()` = 0x8cf5e0 (transfer_manager.cpp, thiscall, ret 8): 9 for an AWOL player (outside national teams); otherwise the first of the 28 rules (+0x8ac) of the continent or nation with the same type answers (virtual +0x18); none: 0;
- `vaga_do_amistoso()` = 0x5c6740 (friendly.cpp, thiscall, ret 0x10): in the friendlies list (+0x34cc), the 25-byte slot of the club with the requested day, year and opponent, or 0;
- `noticia_para_os_tecnicos_do_continente()` = 0x786640 (news.cpp, thiscall, ret 8): continent scope (+0xde = 3) and delivery to every active human manager whose club or nation is in the continent;
- `noticia_para_a_regiao_da_competicao()` = 0x786860 (news.cpp, thiscall, ret 8): to the managers of the competition's nation (0x786520), else of its continent (0x786640), else to all (0x786780);
- `noticia_importante_para_a_pessoa()` = 0x784eb0 (news.cpp, thiscall, ret 4): whether the news is important for the person, by category and type (bids on the person's club, the humans...);
- `texto_do_dado_da_noticia()` = 0x785a20 (news.cpp, thiscall, ret 0xc): copies the text stored from the slot (+5 + 4 × slot) into the buffer (strncpy) and clears the last byte; returns 1;
- `noticia_de_apuro_financeiro()` = 0x74ae60 (media.cpp, thiscall, ret 4): news 0x1790 (id, reputation, draw of 2) to the managers of the nation, or to all for a big club (9000+);
- `olheiros_cobrem_a_nacao()` = 0x7f9190 (scout_manager.cpp, thiscall, ret 8): poor (balance < 1,000,000) and small (reputation < 5000) club outside the nation's region: 0; else 1;
- `fluencia_no_idioma()` = 0x7c6830 (player_stats.cpp, thiscall, ret 0x10): from 0 to 10: the contract level, 10 for a nation (or the second one) speaking the language, or the learned language (binary search);
- `recomecar_historico_da_pessoa()` = 0x86fb80 (staff_records.cpp, thiscall, ret 4): the last offset of the person's history list (+4 of the pair at [+0xc] + 8 × id) = −1 (0x58f110);
- `posicao_na_formacao()` = 0x5b2ba0 (formation.cpp, thiscall, ret 0xc): stores the general or extra position of the slot, the marks, and recomputes the pitch cells without and with the ball (0x5b7e60);
